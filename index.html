Hello, we are Djirian

Good fruit, grown slowly.

We started back in July 2023 with one simple idea. We wanted to spend all our time on a single thing done properly: single-origin freeze-dried durian from the Di Linh highlands of Lâm Đồng, Vietnam.

Our name is Di Linh and Durian stitched together, a small daily reminder of the land that made this fruit what it is. And we put the farmers and the land at the heart of everything we do, with long-term purchase agreements and a price floor that stand behind our partner households, and one promise we won't bend on: No added sugar, no artificial flavors, no blending, no economy version.

Our Sacred Story

Three Million Years,
120 Days, 22 Hours

Three million years ago, the last volcanoes of Di Linh went quiet, leaving three meters of basalt soil. 120 days is the time a durian needs to ripen fully at 950 meters altitude.

22 hours is how long each batch rests in our freeze-drying chamber. These three numbers gather everything we pursue. We do not allow ourselves to shorten any one of them.
