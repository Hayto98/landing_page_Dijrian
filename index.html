From Orchard to You

Seven Steps of Careful Craft

The final quality of a bag of freeze-dried durian is decided almost entirely within the first 48 hours after the fruit leaves the tree. Every action must be precise to the minute.

Harvest & Sort

Step 1: Hand-picked only when naturally ripened on the tree. Harvest teams work in pairs, led by farmers with 20+ years experience. Step 2: Sorted within 4 hours at collection points no more than 5km from the orchard. Step 3: Peeling and pre-processing within 12 hours of harvest at 18-20°C.

Freeze & Dry

Step 4: Flash freezing within 30 minutes at -40°C. Ice crystals form so small they cannot rupture cell walls. Step 5: 22-hour freeze-drying in a vacuum chamber at pressure 2,000 times lower than atmosphere. Ice sublimates directly to vapor without becoming liquid. The cell structure stays intact, the aroma does not vanish.

Our Sacred Story

Three Million Years,
120 Days, 22 Hours

Three million years ago, the last volcanoes of Di Linh went quiet, leaving three meters of basalt soil. 120 days is the time a durian needs to ripen fully at 950 meters altitude.

22 hours is how long each batch rests in our freeze-drying chamber. These three numbers gather everything we pursue. We do not allow ourselves to shorten any one of them.
