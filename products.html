The Growing Region

Di Linh Plateau

The Di Linh Plateau lies in Lâm Đồng Province, at 920–1,050 meters higher than every major commercial durian region of Thailand, Malaysia, and Indonesia. Three rare natural conditions meet here: 3-million-year basalt soil, 8–11°C day-night temperature swing, and a clearly divided wet-dry cycle.
